Credit & Investment Management

Assessment of borrowers, sponsors, projects, cash flows, leverage, repayment capacity, collateral packages, and transaction structures.

Financial Modeling & Pricing
Development of cash-flow models for structured credit instruments, including contractual indexation, interest accrual, amortization schedules, prepayments, and mark-to-market analysis.

Portfolio Monitoring
Monitoring payment performance, construction progress, sales velocity, collateral coverage, financial covenants, reserve accounts, and other credit indicators.

Analysis and negotiation of waivers, amendments, extraordinary amortizations, collateral reinforcement, debt restructuring, and recovery strategies.

Collateral & Recovery Analysis
Evaluation of structures involving fiduciary liens over real estate, receivables, shares/quotas, escrow accounts, and other credit enhancements.

Investment Governance
Preparation of materials and recommendations for investment and risk committees, including analysis of amendments and bondholder resolutions.
